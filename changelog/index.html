0.8.0 Channel point rewards

20 October 2026

  • New Channel point rewards that add PixFray dollars to the redeemer's fighter, up to 5 per channel. The bot confirms each one in chat with the new wallet total. Setting them up from the mod page comes in a later release.
  • New A viewer who redeems without a saved fighter gets their points back. A repeated delivery from Twitch never pays twice.
  • New This changelog page.

0.7.0 Deeper fighter stats

15 October 2026

  • New Upgrade points have no cap. Each level costs more, every stat has a ceiling, and presets spend points for you.
  • New Crit, dodge and counter stats, plus vitality.
  • New Legendary stat rolls with one free reroll, and !stats to see your fighter's numbers in chat.
  • New Turning a picture into a sprite works without AI: PixFray finds the subject and drops the background.
  • Changed Duels are ranked only while the stream is live. Streamers can also limit how often the same two fighters duel in one stream.
  • Fixed The bot no longer stalls on an unreadable shop answer.

0.6.2 Small fixes

12 October 2026

  • Fixed A stray empty slot after full build slots, and the mod page on a channel without an arena.

0.6.1 Rank fixes

9 October 2026

  • Fixed Saving your fighter keeps your rank.
  • Fixed The !fray link opens your channel instead of the channel picker.

0.6.0 Viewer sprites and bot controls

7 October 2026

  • New Turn any picture into a pixel fighter, with an optional AI redraw. Mods review each one before it goes on stream.
  • New !fray off and !fray on silence or wake the bot in a channel.
  • New Mods add the bot's own text commands, with counters, and can turn on a timed !fray reminder.
  • New !give sends dollars (!pay still works), and an unanswered challenge gets an expiry line.
  • Changed The bot names the duel winner in chat, and the overlay no longer shows a winner banner.
  • Changed Duels show 2D hit effects: glow, sparks, shake and a knockout push-in. The overlay option fx=off turns them off, and bubbles=0 hides chat bubbles.
  • Changed Duels with a brand-new Twitch account are unrated, and the same two fighters have a daily ranked limit.
  • Changed The home page is the 3D tour, and the channel picker moved to /play/.
  • Fixed Lead moderators count as mods, and a fighter changed on the website updates on stream.

0.5.0 Open sign-up and the PixFray bot

6 October 2026

  • New Any streamer can turn PixFray on from /start/, with no invite.
  • New The PixFray bot answers duel commands in chat, so streamers need no other chat bot.
  • New Search the channel picker for any channel running PixFray.
  • New Mods can try !checkin for 15 minutes while offline.
  • Changed Shop prices cut to about a third.

0.4.0 PixFray

5 October 2026

  • Changed Mini Chat is now PixFray, and the help command is !fray.
  • New Build slots and cosmetics, on a fighter page with tabs for the shop, Ranks and Rules.
  • New A 3D preview of your fighter, and a 3D tour of how a duel plays out.
  • New !look links straight to picking or changing your fighter.
  • Changed Every page redesigned as a night arena.

0.3.0 Rematches and progression

4 October 2026

  • New !rematch challenges your last opponent.
  • New !checkin and stream streaks.
  • New Dollars, with !wallet, !pay and gifts from mods.
  • New Pets and the shop.
  • Changed Duel results stay out of chat until the stream has shown them.

0.2.0 Upgrades and more fighters

2 October 2026

  • New Upgrades earned from wins (power, guard and luck) and hats to earn.
  • New 56 built-in characters, and up to 24 uploaded characters per channel.
  • New !top and !elo, and a limit on how many fighters show on stream.
  • New Streamer sign-up, with a setup checklist that ticks off each step as it starts working.
  • New Optional duel announcements on the overlay.

0.1.0 First duels

1 October 2026

  • New Chatters walk on stream as pixel fighters through an OBS overlay.
  • New Duels with !challenge @name and !fight, settled by alternating d6 rolls with crits.
  • New Elo, wins and losses saved for every fighter.
  • New The overlay replays each duel: the fighters meet, HP bars drop with each roll, and the loser is knocked out.
  • New A fighter page for viewers and duel controls for mods.
