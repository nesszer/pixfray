Set up PixFray on your Twitch channel

Viewers pick a pixel fighter on this site, then challenge each other with chat commands. The duels play out on your stream through an OBS browser source, and the StreamElements bot posts the results.

Setup takes about 10 minutes, after one Twitch sign-in

You need OBS and the StreamElements bot in your chat. After you sign in, the Stream setup page checks off each step as it starts working.

  1. Browser Source · URLpixfray.xyz/overlay.html?channel=youWidth 1920 · Height 1080
    Add the overlay to OBS Make it a Browser Source at 1920×1080. The setup page gives you the link.
  2. Duel modulePixFray commandsOne bot answers each duel
    Turn off the Duel module It's a built-in StreamElements module. With it off, only one bot answers duel commands.
  3. !challengeExample$(customapi pixfray.xyz/api/se/you/challenge?…)
    Paste 7 commands into StreamElements Each reply is ready to copy: !challenge, !fight, !decline, !rematch, !elo, !ranks and !fray. You can rename them.
  4. Your moderators canExamplePause duelsReset healthReset
    Let your moderators help Optional They can manage fighters, ranks and settings.

PixFray asks Twitch for one permission: your moderator list

What PixFray reads

  • Your public Twitch name, from sign-in
  • Your moderator list (moderation:read), so your mods can open mod controls

What PixFray never does

  • Post in chat as you
  • Change your channel, title or settings
  • See your email or stream key

You can turn PixFray off at any time from the Stream setup page. Fighters and ranks are kept, so turning it back on picks up where you left off.
