Set up Mini Chat on your Twitch channel

Viewers pick a pixel fighter on this site, then challenge each other with chat commands. The duels play out on your stream through an OBS browser source, and the StreamElements bot posts the results.

Checking your invite…

Setup takes about 10 minutes, after one Twitch sign-in

You need OBS and the StreamElements bot in your chat. After you sign in, the Stream setup page checks off each step as it starts working.

  1. Add the overlay to OBS as a Browser Source, 1920×1080. The setup page gives you the link.
  2. Turn off the StreamElements Duel module, so only one bot answers duel commands.
  3. Paste 6 commands into StreamElements. Each reply is ready to copy: !challenge, !fight, !decline, !elo, !ranks and !minichat. You can rename them.
  4. Optional: let your Twitch moderators help with fighters, ranks and settings.

Mini Chat asks Twitch for one permission: your moderator list

Sign-in shares your public Twitch name. The moderation:read permission lets Mini Chat check who your moderators are, so they can open your mod controls. Mini Chat never posts in chat as you and never changes your channel.

You can turn Mini Chat off at any time from the Stream setup page. Fighters and ranks are kept, so turning it back on picks up where you left off.
