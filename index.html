Duel from chat

CommandWhat it does
!challenge @viewerChallenges a viewer. They have 30 s to reply.
!fight / !declineAccepts or turns down a challenge sent to you. Accepting starts the duel right away.

Each swing is one die roll

DieResult
5–6The hit lands and the other fighter is knocked out.
1–2Countered: the other fighter hits back and the swinger is knocked out.
3–4Miss. The other fighter swings next.

The challenger swings first. Hit and counter are equally likely, so going first is no advantage. A duel ends within 8 swings.

Your character appears on stream after your first chat message. The loser is knocked out for a few seconds, and the same two viewers wait 30 s before a rematch.

Add the overlay to OBS

For the streamer. Builds the Browser Source link for the chat overlay.

Open live overlayOpen demo overlay

In OBS, add a Browser Source, paste the link, and set 1920 × 1080 at 30 FPS. The background is transparent. Turn off "Shutdown source when not visible" to stay connected between scenes.
