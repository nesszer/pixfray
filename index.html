Duel from chat

CommandWhat it does
!challenge @viewerChallenges a viewer. They have 30 s to reply.
!accept / !declineAnswers a challenge sent to you.
!attack @viewerUses your default ability.
!strike, !heavy, !healUses that ability. Each has its own cooldown, plus 1 s between any two actions.

Your character appears on stream after your first chat message. Duels left idle for 60 s are cancelled without scoring.

Open live overlayOpen demo overlay

In OBS, add a Browser Source, paste the link, and set 1920 × 1080 at 30 FPS. The background is transparent. Turn off "Shutdown source when not visible" to stay connected between scenes.
