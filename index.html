      Commands

      CommandWhat it does
      !challenge @viewerChallenges a viewer. They have 30 s to reply.
      !fightAccepts a challenge sent to you. The duel is rolled right away and plays out on stream.
      !declineTurns a challenge down.
      !rematchChallenges the last viewer you fought. If they type !rematch too, the duel starts. Rematches open 30 s after a duel.
      !checkinOnce per stream, while it's live: +1 upgrade point. Check in on streams in a row for +1 more at 3, 7, 14 and 30. One missed stream a week doesn't break the streak.
      !walletPosts your PixFray dollars, upgrade points and streak. Each finished duel pays $5 for a win and $3 for a loss.
      !pay @viewer 10Gives dollars to another fighter while the stream is live: up to $100 per stream, after your first 5 duels.
      !petPosts the pet you bring and its boost. Add @viewer to see someone else's.
      !eloPosts your Elo, rank and record in chat. Add @viewer to check someone else.
      !ranksPosts the top 5 of the leaderboard in chat.
      !frayPosts how to join, with a link to this page.

      Duels started from Twitch chat on this channel use the HP fight right now: after !fight, both fighters trade !attack, !strike, !heavy and !heal until one runs out of health. StreamElements commands always use the die rolls.

      Duel rules

      • The challenger swings first, then the fighters take turns.
      • Two critical hits or three hits knock a fighter out.
      • After 12 rolls the fighter with more health wins. If health is level, the next hit or counter decides it.
      • A win without taking any damage is flawless and earns 3 extra Elo.
      • Upgrades change the numbers: each Power point adds 4% to your blows, each Guard point takes 4% off blows you take, and each Luck point gives a 4% chance that a miss still hits.

      The loser is knocked out for a few seconds, and the same two viewers wait 30 s before a rematch.
