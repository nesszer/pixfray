Duels take two chat commands and settle in one die-rolling exchange

Commands

CommandWhat it does
!challenge @viewerChallenges a viewer. They have 30 s to reply.
!fightAccepts a challenge sent to you. The duel is rolled right away and plays out on stream.
!declineTurns a challenge down.

Duels started from Twitch chat on this channel use the HP fight right now: after !fight, both fighters trade !attack, !strike, !heavy and !heal until one runs out of health. StreamElements commands always use the die rolls.

Each swing is one d6 roll

DieResultDamage (% of max HP)
6Critical hit on the other fighter50
5Hit on the other fighter34
3–4Miss0
1–2Countered: the swinger takes the blow34

The challenger swings first, then the fighters take turns. Two critical hits or three hits knock a fighter out. After 12 rolls the fighter with more health wins; if health is level, the next hit or counter decides it. A win without taking any damage is flawless and earns 3 extra Elo.

Your character appears on stream after your first chat message. The loser is knocked out for a few seconds, and the same two viewers wait 30 s before a rematch.
